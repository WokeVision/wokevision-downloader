Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

WokeVision Editor is an internal video publishing tool operated by WokeVision. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

What this tool stores

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the social media accounts WokeVision connects to it (e.g. Instagram, Threads, YouTube, TikTok, X), stored encrypted in a private database.
  • Basic public profile info for the connected accounts (e.g. display name, username) used only to show connection status in the tool's own interface.
  • Video files and captions submitted for editing, stored temporarily to process and publish them.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not collect data from, or act on behalf of, any account other than the ones WokeVision itself explicitly connects.
  • It does not sell, share, or use connected-account data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond publishing the videos WokeVision submits through it.
  • It is not a public-facing service and does not collect data from visitors or the general public.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept only as long as an account stays connected; disconnecting an account removes its stored tokens. Processed video files are retained temporarily for delivery and are not kept indefinitely.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to review or delete stored data, can be sent to contactwokevision@gmail.com.
