Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

WokeVision Editor is a video publishing and analytics tool for creators and pages, operated by WokeVision. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

What this tool stores

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the social media accounts you connect (e.g. Instagram, Threads, YouTube, TikTok, X, Facebook), stored encrypted in a private database.
  • Basic profile info for the connected accounts (e.g. display name, username) used to show connection status.
  • Performance statistics for the connected accounts, such as follower, like and video counts and per-video views, likes, comments and shares (for TikTok, via the user.info.stats and video.list permissions), shown only to you on your own Analytics page.
  • Video files and captions you submit for editing, stored temporarily to process and publish them.
  • Settings you choose, such as saved replies and auto-hide keywords for comments.

Comments and replies

If you use the Comments page, this tool reads the comments and replies on your own posts (on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Threads) through each platform's official API, shows them only to you, and lets you reply to, hide or restore them. Comments are fetched when you open the page and are not kept in this tool's database. Replies and hide actions are sent back to the platform only when you (or an auto-hide keyword you set) trigger them.

Service providers

To run, this tool uses Render (hosting), Neon (database), Cloudflare R2 (temporary video storage) and OpenAI (transcription, captions, and optional reply suggestions). Video audio, captions and, if you ask for a suggested reply, the text of that one comment are sent to OpenAI for that purpose only. These providers act on our behalf and are not allowed to use the data for their own purposes.

Google user data and YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google. WokeVision's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The YouTube data we access (your channel details, your video statistics, and the comments on your own videos) is used only to publish the videos you submit, show you your own statistics, and let you manage comments on your own channel. It is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide these features. You can revoke this tool's access to your Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Threads)

Data received from Meta's APIs (your account details, post statistics, and the comments or replies on your own posts) is used only to provide the publishing, analytics and comment features to you, is not sold or shared, and is not used for advertising or to build profiles of the people who comment.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not collect data from, or act on behalf of, any account other than the ones you explicitly connect.
  • It does not sell or share connected-account data, and does not use it for advertising or any purpose beyond publishing the videos you submit and showing you your own statistics.
  • It does not collect data from website visitors who have not connected an account.

Data retention

Access tokens and statistics are kept only as long as an account stays connected; disconnecting an account removes its stored tokens. Processed video files are retained temporarily for delivery and are not kept indefinitely.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect any account from the Accounts page, which deletes its stored tokens straight away. You can also revoke access from the platform itself (Instagram, Facebook and Threads: Settings, then Apps and websites or Business integrations; YouTube: the Google link above; TikTok and X: their connected-apps settings). To have everything else we hold about you removed, including statistics, settings and any stored videos, email contactwokevision@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days. If you signed in with a Meta platform, this page is also our data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to review or delete stored data, can be sent to contactwokevision@gmail.com.
